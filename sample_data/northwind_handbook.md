# Northwind Robotics Employee Handbook

Northwind Robotics is a fictional company used to demo this application.

## Working Hours and Remote Work

Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in the employee's local time zone. Outside core hours, employees may schedule their day freely. Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Fully remote arrangements require written approval from both the employee's manager and the People Operations team, and are reviewed every six months.

## Paid Time Off

Full-time employees accrue 1.75 days of paid time off (PTO) per month, for a total of 21 days per year. Up to 5 unused PTO days may be carried over into the next calendar year; any additional unused days expire on January 31. PTO requests longer than five consecutive working days must be submitted at least three weeks in advance.

Northwind observes 10 company-wide public holidays. In addition, the office closes between December 24 and January 1, and those days do not count against PTO.

## Parental Leave

Primary caregivers receive 20 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 8 weeks of fully paid leave. Parental leave may be taken in up to two separate blocks within 12 months of the birth or adoption.

## Equipment

Every new employee receives a laptop of their choice (macOS or Linux) and a one-time home-office stipend of 750 USD. Laptops are refreshed every three years. Lost or stolen equipment must be reported to the IT Help Desk within 24 hours via the #it-help channel.

## Expenses

Business expenses under 75 USD do not require pre-approval. Expenses between 75 and 1,000 USD require approval from the employee's manager. Anything above 1,000 USD requires approval from the department's director. Receipts must be submitted in the Expensify tool within 30 days of purchase; reimbursements are paid with the next payroll cycle.

Travel must be booked through the company travel portal. Economy class is required for flights under six hours; premium economy is permitted for flights of six hours or longer.

## Learning and Development

Each employee has an annual learning budget of 1,500 USD, which can be spent on courses, conferences, books, and certifications. Unused learning budget does not roll over. Employees may also take up to 3 paid learning days per year to attend training.

## Performance Reviews

Performance reviews happen twice a year, in April and October. Each review includes a self-assessment, peer feedback from at least two colleagues, and a manager assessment. Compensation adjustments are made once per year following the April review cycle.

## Security

Employees must use the company password manager (1Password) for all work credentials and enable hardware-key two-factor authentication on their Google Workspace account. Security awareness training is mandatory within the first two weeks of employment and annually thereafter. Suspected phishing emails must be reported using the "Report Phish" button in Gmail.

## On-call

Engineers on the platform team participate in a weekly on-call rotation. On-call engineers receive a stipend of 400 USD per week of on-call duty, and must acknowledge pages within 15 minutes. After a night with an incident lasting over two hours between midnight and 6:00, the on-call engineer may start work late the next day.
