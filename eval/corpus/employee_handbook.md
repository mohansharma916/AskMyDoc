# Northwind Robotics Employee Handbook

Northwind Robotics is a fictional company. This handbook is a fixed test corpus for Ask My Docs and does not describe any real employer.

## Working at Northwind

### Working hours

Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in each employee's local time zone. Meetings involving more than two time zones should be scheduled inside the overlap of everyone's core hours. Outside core hours, employees plan their own day. The standard full-time week is 40 hours.

### Remote and hybrid work

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Teams agree on at least one shared office day so people can collaborate in person. Fully remote arrangements require written approval from both the employee's manager and the People Operations team. Fully remote arrangements are reviewed every six months. Employees working from another country for more than 30 days in a calendar year need prior approval from People Operations because of tax and immigration rules.

### Office access

The Lisbon and Toronto offices are open from 07:00 to 21:00 on working days. Badges are personal and must not be shared. Visitors must be registered at reception by their host and accompanied at all times.

## Time Off

### Paid time off

Full-time employees accrue 1.75 days of paid time off (PTO) per month, which is 21 days per year. Part-time employees accrue PTO in proportion to their contracted hours. PTO accrual starts on the first day of employment.

Up to 5 unused PTO days may be carried over into the next calendar year. Any additional unused days expire on January 31 of the following year. Carried-over days are used before newly accrued days.

PTO requests of more than five consecutive working days must be submitted at least three weeks in advance. Shorter requests need one week's notice where possible. Managers respond to PTO requests within three working days.

### Public holidays and winter closure

Northwind observes 10 company-wide public holidays each year, published in January. The offices close between December 24 and January 1. Winter closure days do not count against PTO.

### Sick leave

Employees receive up to 10 paid sick days per year. Sick leave does not carry over. A doctor's note is required for absences longer than three consecutive working days. Employees should tell their manager about a sick day before 10:00 on the day of absence.

### Parental leave

Primary caregivers receive 20 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 8 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Parental leave may be taken in up to two separate blocks within 12 months of the birth or adoption. Employees become eligible for parental leave after three months of employment.

### Bereavement and other leave

Employees receive up to 5 paid days of bereavement leave for the death of an immediate family member and 2 days for other relatives. Jury duty is fully paid. Employees may also take up to 2 paid volunteering days per year.

## Growth and Performance

### Performance reviews

Performance reviews happen twice a year, in April and October. Each review includes a self-assessment, peer feedback from at least two colleagues, and a manager assessment. Compensation adjustments are made once per year, following the April review cycle. Promotions can be proposed in either cycle.

### Probation

New employees have a probation period of six months. During probation the notice period is two weeks for either side. A probation review meeting takes place at the end of month five.

### Resignation and notice periods

After probation, the notice period for resignation is 30 days for individual contributors and 60 days for people managers and directors. Notice must be given in writing to the manager and People Operations. Unused accrued PTO is paid out in the final salary.

## Conduct

### Code of conduct

Northwind expects respectful, inclusive behaviour in every setting, including chat, video calls and company events. Harassment, discrimination and retaliation are not tolerated. Concerns can be raised with a manager, with People Operations, or anonymously through the Ethics Line.

### Conflicts of interest

Employees must disclose any outside employment, board seat, or investment in a supplier or competitor to People Operations before accepting it. Gifts from suppliers worth more than 50 USD must be declined or reported.
