Travel and Expenses Policy

This policy explains how Northwind Robotics employees book business travel and claim business expenses. Northwind is a fictional company used as a test corpus.

Expense approvals

Business expenses under 75 USD do not require pre-approval. Expenses between 75 and 1,000 USD require approval from the employee's manager before purchase. Expenses above 1,000 USD require approval from the department director.

Software subscriptions of any amount must also be reviewed by the IT team before purchase, so that security and licensing can be checked.

Submitting receipts

Receipts must be submitted in the Expensify tool within 30 days of purchase. Claims submitted later than 90 days after purchase are not reimbursed. Approved expenses are reimbursed with the next payroll cycle.

If a receipt is lost, the employee must submit a missing-receipt declaration signed by their manager. Missing-receipt declarations are limited to three per calendar year.

Booking travel

All flights, trains and hotels must be booked through the company travel portal, TravelDesk. Bookings should be made at least 14 days before departure where possible.

Flights

  • Economy class is required for flights shorter than six hours.

  • Premium economy is permitted for flights of six hours or longer.
  • Business class requires approval from a vice president and is only allowed for flights longer than ten hours.

Hotels

The nightly hotel limit is 180 USD in most cities and 250 USD in New York, London, Tokyo and Zurich. Employees may stay with friends or family instead and claim a flat 40 USD per night.

Ground transport and mileage

Public transport and taxis are reimbursed. Rental cars need manager approval. Use of a personal car for business travel is reimbursed at 0.55 USD per kilometre.

Meals and per diem

Trip typeDaily meal allowance
Domestic travel60 USD
International travel85 USD

Alcohol is not reimbursed, except at client dinners approved by a director. Team celebrations follow the team-events budget of 50 USD per person per quarter.

Corporate cards

Employees who travel more than four times a year can request a corporate card. Corporate cards must never be used for personal purchases. Personal charges made by mistake must be repaid within 15 days.
